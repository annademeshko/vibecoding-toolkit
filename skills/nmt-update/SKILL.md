## Required methodology

Source: https://github.com/zamesin/Next-Move-Theory-Canon-and-Skills/tree/f8e87d10255b48d381ebc7fa65172960eb6d483f/Next-Move-Theory-Canon. Original methodology by Ivan Zamesin.

A Job describes a desired transition from State A to State B toward a higher-level Job. Segment primarily by similar Core Jobs, measurable success criteria and their priority order. Measure value against customers' current alternatives and six costs (money, time, effort, cognitive load, negative emotions, Tax Jobs). Prefer subtractive mechanics and repair of the Critical Chain of Jobs over speculative feature accumulation.

Mark claims as Evidence (cite source and sample), Derived (show logic) or Hypothesis (needs testing). Treat sparse data as validation debt. Design a Riskiest Assumption Test with audience, behavior, criterion for pass/fail and consequences of failure. With weak support use GO (to validation), never claim the feature is proven to work.

Do not invent interviews, market size, adoption, conversion, revenue or customer proof. For exact methodology and deep cases read the original canon; the public content is only part of the complete NMT framework.
